[ 01 / WHAT IS THIS ]

      
Not a brand. Not a gallery. An art project living inside Saigon.

      
Ordinary objects are borrowed from the street, moved slightly, sometimes transformed, then put back into the real city.

    

    

      
[ 02 / METHOD ]

      
THE CITY
IS THE
STUDIO.

      
Studio, material, set, gallery and character at the same time. Every intervention is photographed, filmed, written down.
