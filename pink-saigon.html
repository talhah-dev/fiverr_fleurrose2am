PINK SAIGON

AN ONGOING CREATIVE PRACTICE & LABEL FOUNDED BY FLEUR ROSE

Pink Saigon is a living, evolving ecosystem between contemporary fine art, street installation, objects, and social situations in Ho Chi Minh City. Not a corporate brand — an artistic quest for everyday beauty and connection.

PROJECTS IN DEVELOPMENT · 2026
001 · URBAN INSTALLATION IN DEVELOPMENT

SAY IT

A rug, a parasol, two stools. A place on the pavement where strangers can sit down and talk.

ARTIFACTS: TALK TO ME postcard, magnetic letter set.
002 · PHOTOGRAPHY IDEA

100 STRANGERS

One hundred people met on the street. One frame each, nothing staged.

ARTIFACTS: Numbered print series.
003 · OBJECT / SCULPTURE PROTOTYPE

PINK STOOL

The Vietnamese plastic stool becomes an icon. Object, then sculpture, then character.

ARTIFACTS: Miniature stool, pin, plush.
004 · NOMADIC EXHIBITION IN DEVELOPMENT

COLLECTION NOMADE

Works placed temporarily in cafes, hotels and shared spaces. The venue becomes a micro-gallery.

ARTIFACTS: Host card, exhibition poster.
