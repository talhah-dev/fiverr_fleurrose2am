THE SECOND FUTURE

Some places never leave us.

SAIGON, NOVEMBER 2008 — 2026 · FLEUR ROSE

SEE THE PHOTOGRAPHS ↓ SCALE SIMULATION
THE STORY

I have spent a lot of time in Saigon. In November 2008, somewhere in five years of travelling, I was there with a camera. The photographs went onto a hard drive. The hard drive went into a drawer.

In 2026 I came back to the city. The same year, I found the drive again. Eighteen years. I don’t know what became of the people in these pictures. I know they were there, and so was I.
